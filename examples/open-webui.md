# Use Mervex in Open WebUI

Mervex is fully OpenAI-compatible, so Open WebUI connects in under a minute.

1. Open **Open WebUI → Settings → Connections → OpenAI API**
2. Set:
   - **URL:** `https://api.mervex.cc.cd/v1`
   - **Key:** your Mervex key (`Mervex-ai-...`) — get one free at https://mervex.cc.cd/auth
3. Click **Save**, then refresh the model list — 100+ `Msrfteam/...` models appear.
